Hermes Devbox WHOOP Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-24

Hermes Devbox is a private assistant operated by Artur Do Lago for his own connected accounts. This policy covers its WHOOP connection. The separate Google privacy policy continues to govern Google account data.

Data accessed and purpose

With the owner's authorization, Hermes reads data through the official WHOOP API v2 using a community adapter installed locally and pinned to a reviewed version. The adapter is not an official WHOOP product. Access depends on the permissions granted and includes:

  • Recovery: recovery scores, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, calibration status, and related available measurements such as blood oxygen and skin temperature.
  • Sleep: sleep and wake times, sleep stages and duration, sleep need, performance, consistency, and available respiratory measurements.
  • Physiological cycles and workouts: times, strain, activity types, and available activity measurements.
  • Account profile: account identity, name and email when needed to confirm the intended WHOOP account.

The connection is read-only. It uses OAuth authorization and refresh tokens to perform authorized reads over time; it does not need to retain the owner's WHOOP password or change records in WHOOP.

Data supports the owner's questions and periodic wellness summaries, including sleep and recovery reviews and comparisons with the owner's own history. These are periodic checks, not continuous monitoring or an emergency service. Missing, delayed, revised or unscored data can limit a summary. Hermes does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment through this connection.

AI processing and private delivery

Relevant WHOOP data and task instructions may be sent to the configured AI service, currently OpenAI, to generate the owner's summaries and responses. Relevant results may be delivered to the owner's configured private Telegram destination. Data may also appear in the owner's local assistant history or notes when used in a requested task. The public website does not receive or display these records.

Artur has confirmed that sharing for model training is disabled for the OpenAI account used by Hermes. This is the owner's confirmation, not an independent inspection of the provider's settings, and it is not a promise of zero provider retention. The operator must check these settings when changing the provider or account. OpenAI and Telegram process transmitted content under their applicable terms and account settings.

Hermes Devbox does not sell WHOOP data or use it for advertising or data brokerage. Processing is limited to the owner's requested assistant functions and configured wellness routines; it does not make health information public or send it to other recipients without the owner's instructions.

Local storage, backups and retention

OAuth credentials, including refresh tokens, are stored locally on the owner's devbox with restricted file permissions. The adapter has no separate data cache, has telemetry disabled, and communicates with WHOOP's official authentication and API services without a cloud relay. Returned data and summaries may persist in assistant history, requested notes, task state or private verification records. This policy does not promise that every local file is encrypted.

The WHOOP connection's credentials and local data stores are outside the personal backup inclusion list. Shared assistant histories that can contain personal and work information are also excluded. A summary later saved into a note separately classified and approved for personal backup may be included in encrypted backups. Such copies follow the applicable backup retention policy rather than disappearing when the connection is revoked.

There is no universal automatic expiration currently configured for local WHOOP records or related assistant history. They remain until the owner removes them or changes retention settings. Provider-held copies follow the providers' applicable retention policies and controls.

Disconnecting and deleting data

The owner can disconnect the integration in WHOOP's connected-app controls to revoke future API access. Disconnecting does not delete previously fetched local data, generated notes, assistant history, delivered Telegram messages or copies held by an AI provider. It does not delete the original records in WHOOP.

To remove retained app data, the owner can instruct the operator to stop WHOOP routines and remove its local credentials, cached records, task state and affected history or notes. If approved notes entered backups, their retained backup copies must also be addressed. Copies transmitted to OpenAI or Telegram must be addressed through those services' available controls. The operator should preserve unrelated information and confirm what was removed and what remains.
