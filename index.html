Hermes Devbox

A private assistant for Artur Do Lago's own Google accounts.

Hermes Devbox helps Artur find and summarize email, draft and send messages, manage calendar events, and work with files, Google Docs and Google Sheets. Read-only contact access helps identify the intended recipients. Each account is connected separately through Google's consent screen.

The work Google Chat connection supports reading conversations and sending explicitly requested messages. Proactive routines help track commitments, prepare meetings and deliver private reminders, daily updates and weekly reviews.

Artur controls the assistant through his private devbox and messaging connection. Defined routines can conservatively organize new inbox mail and send a daily brief to his configured private Telegram chat. Archiving actions are recorded so they can be undone. The assistant can also act on Artur's direct requests.

Hermes Devbox is operated by Artur Do Lago for his own use. It is not a public sign-up service. Google account data is used to provide these requested assistant features, subject to the Privacy Policy.
