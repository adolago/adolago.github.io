Hermes Devbox Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-23

Hermes Devbox is a private assistant operated by Artur Do Lago for his own connected accounts. This policy explains how it handles Google account data when providing requested tasks and defined routines.

Google data accessed

Access depends on the permissions granted separately for each connected account and the task being performed:

  • Account identity: account email and authentication information needed to connect the intended account.
  • Gmail: message headers, labels, snippets, message content, drafts and attachments when needed for the requested task. The assistant can search and summarize mail, prepare drafts, send messages, and change labels or archive mail.
  • Google Chat (work account): space metadata and messages needed to identify requests, track follow-ups and prepare work. The assistant can read spaces and messages and send messages when Artur instructs it. Incoming messages do not independently authorize replies. Chat permissions do not include deleting messages or administering spaces.
  • Calendar: event details, times, descriptions, locations and attendees needed to read, create, update or delete events.
  • Drive, Docs and Sheets: file metadata and relevant document, spreadsheet or other file contents needed to find, read, create, organize or edit the requested material.
  • Contacts: names, email addresses and other relevant contact details used to resolve recipients. Contacts access is read-only.

The app does not request permission to change Gmail mailbox settings or permanently delete email messages. API permissions may cover more information than an individual task requires; access is directed by Artur's requests and the configured routines.

Purpose and automated actions

Data is used to provide the assistant features described above. The daily inbox routine uses message metadata and snippets to make conservative classifications. Its safeguards retain protected or uncertain messages in the inbox, label priority items, and archive qualifying promotions or newsletters. It records its actions and prior label state so its changes can be undone. Classification can be mistaken; Artur can review and correct it.

Artur has authorized the assistant to send mail, archive mail, create or change events, and edit documents in response to his requests and defined routines without repeated per-action confirmation. Ambiguous account choices or recipients require clarification. These permissions do not authorize unrelated actions.

Defined proactive routines may check calendars, mail, work Chat and saved commitments, record pending actions, prepare meetings and deliver reminders or reviews privately to Artur, including overnight. Checks are periodic and bounded; they do not guarantee immediate detection or complete historical coverage. Personal commitments remain separate from work records and mixed assistant history.

AI processing and disclosure

Relevant Google data and task instructions may be sent to the configured AI service to generate summaries, drafts and proposed actions. The current primary AI service is OpenAI. Only processors and account settings compatible with the commitments in this policy may be used for Google data.

Hermes Devbox does not use Google Workspace API data to develop, improve or train non-personalized or generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. It does not transfer Google data for those purposes.

Google data is not sold or used by Hermes Devbox for advertising, data brokerage, creditworthiness decisions or lending. Transfers are limited to providing the requested functionality: Google for account operations and selected encrypted backups; the configured AI processor for inference; Telegram for the owner's requested briefs and replies; and recipients or collaborators involved in an action Artur authorizes. A daily brief is addressed only to Artur's configured private Telegram destination. Telegram processes delivered content under its own terms; the same applies to messages or documents Artur chooses to share with recipients.

Local storage and protection

Google credentials and refresh tokens are stored on Artur's devbox. Personal and work accounts use separate token stores, with encrypted file keyrings and restricted local unlock files. Local access permissions protect configuration, operational records and task state. The assistant may retain fetched material, generated output, conversation history and action records locally to perform tasks, preserve context and support undo. Shared assistant history can contain information from more than one account.

Selected personal setup data, personal Google credentials and any included personal account state are backed up in encrypted form to Artur's personal Google Drive. Work account data, work credentials, mixed assistant history and WhatsApp archives are excluded from that personal backup. Obsidian vaults are excluded unless separately classified and approved for inclusion. This policy does not promise that every local file is encrypted or that third-party services retain no data.

Retention and deletion

Connected credentials remain until revoked or removed. Local task records and assistant history have no universal automatic expiration currently configured; they remain until Artur deletes them or changes the relevant retention settings. The personal backup policy retains up to seven daily, four weekly and six monthly recovery points; these can overlap. Older included data may therefore remain recoverable in retained snapshots after deletion from the live devbox. AI-service and Telegram retention depend on their applicable terms and account settings; this policy does not claim immediate deletion from those services.

Artur can disconnect Hermes Devbox in his Google Account's third-party connections settings to revoke future API access. Revocation alone does not erase data already fetched, generated, sent or backed up.

To remove locally retained app data, Artur can instruct the operator to stop the relevant routines and remove the affected account's credentials, cached material, task records and associated assistant history. The operator must also remove affected retained backup snapshots, or explain the remaining backup-retention period, and address copies held by the AI service or Telegram through those services' available controls. This process should preserve unrelated account data and confirm what was actually removed. Original mail, events and files in Google remain unless Artur separately requests their deletion; messages already delivered to another recipient cannot be erased from that recipient's account by disconnecting this app.

Public website

GitHub Pages hosts these public information pages. Hermes adds no analytics, advertising, sign-in form or tracking cookies to this website. GitHub processes website requests under its Privacy Statement. The public website does not provide access to connected Google accounts.

Changes

This policy will be updated when material data-handling practices change. The current version and effective date will remain available at this page. New uses or permissions will be disclosed and authorized before they are introduced.
